Overview

SenseCart is a mobile product concept designed to improve the shopping experience through personalized digital support. The project combines market research, product strategy, brand development, business planning, and mobile interface design into a single concept built from the ground up.

The Opportunity

Shoppers are often left to compare products, prices, and reviews across scattered apps and browser tabs, with little guidance tailored to what they actually need. The opportunity was to design a single mobile experience that acts as a personalized shopping companion — reducing decision fatigue while keeping the process fast and trustworthy.

Audience & Market Insights

Early research focused on how people currently make shopping decisions online: which signals they trust, where they get stuck, and what makes them abandon a purchase. These insights shaped the product's core value proposition and positioning against existing shopping and comparison apps.

User Flow

Interface Design

UI design focused on a clean, low-friction mobile interface — surfacing the right information at each step without overwhelming the shopper.

Business Model

Business planning covered how SenseCart could sustain itself — considering affiliate and partnership revenue, positioning against existing shopping tools, and a phased go-to-market approach.

Key Takeaways

SenseCart pushed me to think beyond interface design alone — tying product decisions back to a viable business model and a clear market position. Building the strategy and brand foundation first made every later design decision easier to justify.
