04 · Campaign Strategy

SHINHAN SECURITIES
GEN Z CAMPAIGN

Translating financial and compliance-heavy topics into approachable digital content for a Gen Z audience.

Role

Campaign Strategy, Financial Content, Brand Communication

Timeline

2025

Organization

Shinhan Securities

Tools

Figma, Canva, Claude, ChatGPT, Adobe

Deliverables

Content Strategy, Digital Assets, Campaign Copy

Overview

Shinhan Securities is a major South Korean financial institution. I worked on a digital content campaign aimed at making financial literacy and investment concepts accessible to a younger, Gen Z audience — translating complex regulatory and compliance-heavy topics into content that felt relatable and clear.

The Opportunity

Financial services brands often struggle to connect with younger audiences because their content defaults to jargon and formality. The opportunity was to bridge that gap — maintaining regulatory accuracy while building content that Gen Z would actually stop for.

Strategy & Approach

Simplify without losing accuracy

  • Rewrote compliance-heavy copy into plain, conversational language
  • Used analogies and relatable scenarios to explain financial concepts
  • Maintained all regulatory requirements while improving readability

Design for the Gen Z scroll

  • Led with hook-driven headlines that connected to everyday situations
  • Used bold typography and minimal visuals over dense charts
  • Short-form formats (carousels, short video) matched platform behavior

Brand communication

  • Balanced Shinhan's formal brand voice with a warmer, more approachable tone
  • Proposed tone-of-voice guidelines for Gen Z content

Selected Work

Project Designs

Planned campaign designs translating financial concepts for a Gen Z audience.

Takeaway

Working within a regulated industry taught me that creative constraints can be a creative advantage. When you can't say something loosely, you find smarter ways to say it clearly. This project expanded my range as a content strategist beyond startup and community work into institutional brand communication.
