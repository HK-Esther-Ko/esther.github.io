05 · Editorial & Content Operations

KOSDAQ EDITORIAL
WORKFLOW

Developing content and editorial workflows for KOSDAQ-listed companies, managing structured publishing across multiple brands.

Role

Editorial Workflow, Content Operations, Brand Communication

Timeline

2024

Organization

KOSDAQ Listed Companies Association

Tools

Notion, Claude, Adobe, Content Management Systems

Deliverables

Editorial System, Content Templates, Publishing Workflow

Overview

At the KOSDAQ Listed Companies Association, I developed editorial workflows and content systems to support communication across multiple listed companies. The work involved building structured processes for consistent, high-quality publishing at scale.

The Opportunity

Managing content for multiple institutional brands requires structure, not just creativity. The opportunity was to build editorial systems that reduced production time while improving consistency and quality across all output.

Strategy & Approach

Editorial system design

  • Mapped existing content workflows to identify bottlenecks
  • Designed a structured editorial calendar across multiple brands
  • Created content templates for recurring formats

Content operations

  • Standardized writing and review process for institutional communication
  • Maintained brand voice consistency across different organizations
  • Coordinated publishing schedule across multiple teams

Takeaway

Working in an institutional environment at scale taught me that good systems are what make good content sustainable. Building the editorial infrastructure was as important as the writing itself — and the ability to work within formal organizational structures while maintaining content quality is a skill I carried into every project after.
