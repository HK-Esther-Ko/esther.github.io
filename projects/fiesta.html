01 · Social Media Strategy

FIESTA SOCIAL
MEDIA GROWTH

Turning an offline founder community into a consistent and measurable social presence.

Role

Social Strategy, Content Design, Copywriting, Video Editing

Timeline

June – August 2026

Organization

FIESTA — Austin Founder Community

Tools

Figma, Canva, CapCut, Claude, ChatGPT, Photoshop

Deliverables

Content Strategy, 3–4 Weekly Posts, Reels, Carousels

Overview

FIESTA is an Austin-based founder community built around monthly networking events and founder storytelling. I independently managed its Instagram presence, creating 3–4 weekly content pieces across carousels, Reels, event promotions, and recurring founder-focused series.

The Opportunity

FIESTA had an active offline community and a large library of founder stories, but needed a more consistent content structure, a recognizable visual identity, and a repeatable social workflow that could be maintained week over week.

Strategy & Approach

Built recurring content pillars

  • Founder WINs — celebrating milestones and achievements
  • Founder Spotlight — in-depth creator and story profiles
  • Founder Motivation — short-form inspirational content
  • Interviews — video conversations with community members
  • Event Promotions — pre and post-event content coverage

Improved content hooks

  • Short, punchy cover copy that leads with the outcome
  • First slide delivers the core message before any scroll
  • Real founder photos over stock, building trust and recognition

Connected offline and online experiences

  • On-site interviews and video capture at monthly events
  • Live event photography for real-time posting
  • Pre and post-event content sequences to drive attendance and recap

Process

Research→ Story Selection→ Hook & Copy→ Design in Figma/Canva→ Video Edit in CapCut→ Review & Publish→ Performance Analysis

I used Claude and ChatGPT to speed up hook generation and caption drafts, then refined each piece to match FIESTA's voice before publishing.

Results

Metric Before During Management Change
Views 6.7K 13.5K +103%
Reach 958 3.15K +229%
Interactions 193 525 +172%

Created all top-performing posts and Reels during the reporting period, including content reaching up to 2.3K views. Non-follower reach consistently improved with stronger hooks and human-centered storytelling.

Takeaway

Clearer hooks and stronger human storytelling consistently improved non-follower reach. I used performance insights to refine each new content series rather than treating publishing as the final step — treating analytics as creative feedback, not just reporting.
